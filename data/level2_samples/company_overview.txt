# TechNova Inc. — Company Overview

TechNova Inc. was founded in 2018 by Sarah Chen and Marcus Williams in San Francisco, California.
The company specializes in enterprise AI solutions for the healthcare and finance sectors.

## Mission
TechNova's mission is to make AI accessible to every enterprise, regardless of technical expertise.

## Core Products
1. NovaSense — Real-time anomaly detection for financial transactions
2. NovaHealth — Clinical decision support system for hospitals
3. NovaPlatform — Low-code AI pipeline builder for enterprise teams

## Revenue Milestones
- 2020: $1.2M ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
- 2021: $3.5M ARR
- 2022: $8.1M ARR
- 2023: $15.4M ARR
- 2024: $28.7M ARR (86% YoY growth)

## Team
TechNova employs 187 people across three offices: San Francisco, London, and Singapore.
The engineering team (94 people) is led by CTO Dr. Aiko Tanaka, a former Google Brain researcher.

## Technology Stack
The core platform is built on Python, Kubernetes, and PostgreSQL.
AI models are deployed using ONNX Runtime for cross-platform inference.
